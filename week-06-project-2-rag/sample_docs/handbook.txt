1. RETURNS & REFUNDS

1.1 Return Window
All products may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must
be in its original packaging, unused, and with all tags attached. Items returned
after 30 days will not be accepted.

1.2 How to Initiate a Return
To start a return, email returns@acmecorp.com with your order number and the
reason for the return. Our team will reply within 2 business days with a prepaid
shipping label. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any authorized shipping
location.

1.3 Refund Processing
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the
returned item. The refund will be issued to the original payment method. Please
note that shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our
error.

1.4 Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges at this time. To get a different item, please
return the original and place a new order.

1.5 Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- Gift cards
- Downloadable software
- Personalized or custom-made products
- Items marked as "Final Sale"

2. SHIPPING & DELIVERY

2.1 Shipping Options
We offer three shipping tiers:
- Standard (3-5 business days): $5.99
- Express (1-2 business days): $14.99
- Overnight (next business day): $24.99

Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

2.2 Order Processing
Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days are processed the same day.
Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on weekends are processed the next business day.

2.3 Tracking Your Order
A tracking number is emailed to you once your order ships. You can also check
your order status by logging into your account on our website.

2.4 International Shipping
We ship to over 40 countries. International shipping rates and delivery times
vary by destination. Import duties and taxes are the responsibility of the
buyer and are not included in the shipping cost.

2.5 Lost or Damaged Packages
If your package arrives damaged, take photos and contact us within 48 hours.
We will send a replacement at no extra cost. If your tracking shows delivered
but you did not receive the package, contact us within 7 days and we will
investigate.

3. PRODUCT WARRANTY

3.1 Warranty Coverage
All products are covered by a one-year limited warranty against manufacturing
defects. This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal
use conditions.

3.2 What Is Not Covered
The warranty does not cover:
- Damage from misuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents)
- Damage caused by third-party accessories

3.3 How to File a Warranty Claim
Email warranty@acmecorp.com with your order number, a description of the
defect, and photos showing the issue. We will review your claim within 5
business days and provide a resolution.

3.4 Warranty Resolution
If your warranty claim is approved, we will either repair the item, replace it,
or issue a refund at our discretion. Replacement items carry the remainder of
the original warranty period.

4. PAYMENT & BILLING

4.1 Accepted Payment Methods
We accept the following payment methods:
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Acme Corp gift cards

4.2 Payment Security
All transactions are encrypted using TLS 1.3. We do not store your full credit
card number on our servers. Payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is
PCI-DSS compliant.

4.3 Billing Issues
If you notice an incorrect charge, contact billing@acmecorp.com within 30 days.
Include your order number and a description of the discrepancy.

5. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

5.1 Contact Us
- Email: support@acmecorp.com
- Phone: 1-800-ACME-123 (Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST)
- Live chat: Available on our website during business hours

6. PRIVACY & DATA

6.1 What We Collect
We collect the information necessary to process your orders: name, email,
shipping address, and payment information.

6.2 How We Use Your Data
Your data is used to:
- Process and ship your orders
- Send order confirmations and shipping updates
- Improve our products and services

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6.3 Data Deletion
You may request deletion of your account and personal data by emailing
privacy@acmecorp.com. We will process your request within 30 days, subject to
legal retention requirements.
